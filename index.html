CPR Coach

CPR targets

Rate: 100–120 pushes per minute. Follow the beat.
Depth: 2–2.4 inches.
Every 30 pushes: 2 breaths if trained, otherwise keep pushing.
Every 2 minutes: switch rescuers if someone can take over.

Training aid, not a medical device. Call 911 first.

Set up the phone

  1. Call 911 and put it on speaker.
  2. Put the phone about 3 ft in front of you so your face is facing your phone screen.
  3. Try to get whole body in frame, head to knees, and light in front of you if possible.

Don't spend more than 10 seconds here. If alone, start pushing.
